Your repair shop is already listed on Fix-It Registry. Claim it to take bookings, set your prices and manage your calendar.

    Find your listing

    Search by name or postcode. We already list most UK repair shops from public records.

    Take bookings

    Once approved you get the calendar, your prices, your services and every request.
