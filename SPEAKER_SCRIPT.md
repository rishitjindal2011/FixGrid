>

>

## Slide 2: The Painful Reality (The Problem)
* **Slide Title:** `Getting Things Fixed is a Nightmare`
* **Timing:** `25s`
* **Visual:** 3 problem cards: Zero Warranty, Opaque Pricing, and Safety/Quality Anxiety.

### 🎙️ Word-for-Word Script:
> "Today, getting anything repaired is stressful for three major reasons:
> 
> 1. **Zero Warranty**: If you look at the market leader's public pages today, there is virtually no guaranteed warranty. If your device breaks down 24 hours later, you have no recourse.
> 2. **Opaque Pricing**: Customers face arbitrary diagnostic charges and on-the-spot price inflation.
> 3. **Unvetted Quality**: You have no idea who is opening up your expensive electronics or entering your home.
> 
> Over 78% of consumers delay repairs simply because they don't know who to trust."

## Slide 3: The Big Idea (The Solution)
* **Slide Title:** `The FixGrid Solution: Trust as a Service`
* **Timing:** `25s`
* **Visual:** 3 Hero Pillars: Guaranteed Warranty, Safe Escrow, Verified Local Heroes.

### 🎙️ Word-for-Word Script:
> "FixGrid transforms trust from an afterthought into the core product:
> 
> - **Guaranteed Warranty**: Every completed repair comes with a mandatory 30 to 90-day platform-backed warranty.
> - **Safe Escrow Payments**: When a customer books, their money is held safely in escrow. The technician only gets paid once the customer verifies the repair is working.
> - **Verified Local Heroes**: We verify every technician through government IDs, business registrations, and physical storefront checks before they can accept a single job."

# 💡 Pro-Tips for Hackathon Demo & Pitching

1. **Focus on the Story**: Start with a relatable scenario ("Ever had your phone screen crack before an interview?").
2. **Show the Working Product**: When answering questions, flip to the live app screens (Customer map, Technician Workbench, Admin Dispute console) to prove it's fully built.
3. **Emphasize the Wedge (Warranty + Escrow)**: When asked *"Why won't Urban Company just crush you?"*, answer:
   > *"Urban Company is a centralized aggregator with high take-rates and generic contractors. FixGrid is an open infrastructure platform empowering the existing 500,000 local repair shops with trust tools, escrow, and guaranteed warranties that aggregators don't offer."*
